# Increment 221 — Historical Statistics Integration

Wires the validated Increment 220 frozen Paper historical baseline into the
mobile Statistics > Historical tab.

Historical is organized into three sections:
- Production Strategy Baseline: A / C5
- Research Baseline: B
- Frozen Paper Historical: all 8 frozen Paper segments

Each Paper historical card shows the frozen 2024–2026 resolved metrics:
Trades, W/L, Win Rate, Expectancy, Profit Factor, Total R, and Max Drawdown.
Cards expand to show the 2025 and 2026 breakdown without crowding the page.

No Historical values are merged with Production Forward or Paper Forward.
Increment 219.1 independent-exposure filtering for forward statistics is
unchanged.
